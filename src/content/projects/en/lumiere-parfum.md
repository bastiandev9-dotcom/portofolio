A native PHP e-commerce store built on a 19-table MySQL schema — product catalog with filters and sorting, cart, checkout with courier and promo handling, order tracking by code, and a full admin panel with sales reports, order status updates, and CSV/PDF export.

Authentication uses bcrypt with token-based password reset. The whole thing runs on XAMPP with no framework.
