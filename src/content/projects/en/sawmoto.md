A decision support system that ranks Honda motorcycles with Simple Additive Weighting — 16 models scored against three weighted criteria: price, tank capacity, and engine displacement.

Users pick a set of bikes, then weight each criterion on a 1–5 slider. The engine normalises every value (max-normalisation for benefit, min-normalisation for cost), applies the weights, and ranks the fleet. Add or remove motor rows as the list grows.
